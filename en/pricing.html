03 Glossary

What these
words mean

What actually lands in the chat under each name from the table.

Every morning

Morning

A message the bot writes on its own, without being asked: how the night went, where recovery stands, what the training week has looked like and what follows from that for today. The comparison runs against your own norm, built from your past weeks — not against an average athlete.

Any hour

"Today"

The same morning, reopened with a button in the afternoon or evening. Nothing is recalculated — you see exactly the message that arrived in the morning, with all its numbers and the coach's words.

After a workout

Breakdown

Arrives on its own, as soon as the workout comes off the watch. Numbers and calculations always: pace, heart rate by zone, how the work held up from start to finish. The coach's words — what those numbers mean for you specifically — three times a week on Basic, in every breakdown on Pro.

On Basic the words go to the first three workouts of the week, whichever they happen to be. Train five or six times and the Sunday long run arrives as bare numbers. If the long work is the one that matters most in your week, that's the argument for Pro.

Your question

Questions to the coach

An ordinary chat: "I'd hate to break the week — intervals anyway?" — and an answer with reasoning, not the same number restated. The coach remembers your history, your plan and your past conversations.

Sunday

Weekly wrap-up

Sunday evening — what the seven days came to: how much of what was done, how it sits against previous weeks, where the load is heading. It arrives on your local time, not on Moscow's.

Always open

"My numbers"

The /data command: everything that came off the watch, as it is. Next to it /memory — what the coach remembers about you and what you can erase — and /team, who can see your data and how to revoke that.

04 The rules

Payment, renewal
and switching

In plain words. In full and in legal form — in the terms of service (in Russian).

How to pay

The bot sends the payment link itself — and asks first which card you have: issued by a Russian bank or a foreign one. The first goes through YooKassa, the second through Robokassa. The payment page belongs to the processor; there is nowhere to pay on the site, and we never see your card details.

The period counts from the moment access opens, not from the charge: time spent sorting something out isn't time you lose.

No auto-charges

Money is taken once, for the period you chose. We have no right to charge the card again, and nothing "renews itself": the period ends, access closes. There is nothing to cancel.

So it doesn't come as a surprise, the bot reminds you twice — three days before and one day before.

How the counters work

The week is a calendar one, Monday through Sunday; the day runs midnight to midnight. Both in your own time zone, not Moscow's.

They are spent in order: the coach's words go to the first three workouts of the week, not the best three. When the count runs out the bot says so plainly and names when it resets. The breakdown still arrives — without the coach's words, but with the numbers and the calculations.

Switching levels

Paid days never burn, in any case. Same plan — the new period is added to what's left. Upgrading — it turns on at once, and the remainder is converted into days of the new level by the ratio of the monthly prices: 20 days of Basic cost the same as 10 days of Pro, so a month of Pro becomes forty days — 10 converted plus 30 new.

Downgrading — the old level lives out everything paid for, the new one starts after. Nothing to pay on top.

Refunds

You can stop at any point. The refund is proportional to unused days: amount paid ÷ days in the period × full days remaining. Ten days into a 999 ₽ month of Pro — 666 ₽ back. Two months into a 9,990 ₽ year of Pro — about 8,320 ₽ back.

An email to hello@fizkult.ai with your @username and the payment date is enough. The refund takes up to 10 days, and processing fees are not deducted from it.

If the watch goes quiet

The bot will say so itself, rather than pretend everything is fine. The period keeps running — but if what you paid for was unavailable for more than two days in a row through our fault or Garmin's, write to us: we extend it by the length of the gap.

Two caveats, both from the terms: write within the paid period or within 14 days after it, and a gap doesn't count as ours if the watch simply didn't sync or if you revoked the connection yourself.

05 Questions

What people ask
about the plans

Which one should I take if I don't know?

Don't choose now — the week is free and complete. Two numbers decide it, and you'll know both within seven days: how often you train, and on which days you write to the coach.

Three or four workouts a week — the Basic count covers them, every one gets its words. Five or more — two or three workouts won't get them, and that's the real argument for Pro, not the unlimited chat. Questions are simpler: three a day doesn't catch the people who write a lot, it catches the people who take one day apart in pieces — sleep in the morning, pace after the workout, tomorrow in the evening. If that's you, Pro too.

What exactly do I lose on Basic?

Two things. The first is the weekly wrap-up: Basic doesn't have it at all. The second isn't a feature but a frequency: the coach's words arrive three times a week rather than in every breakdown, and chat questions are three a day.

The breakdowns themselves arrive after every workout on both levels, with numbers and calculations. On Basic, past the count, they simply have no living text in them.

If I pay for a year and it isn't for me a month later?

We refund what you haven't used. The rule is the same for a month and for a year: amount ÷ days in the period × full days remaining. Two months into a 9,990 ₽ year of Pro, about 8,320 ₽ comes back; six months in, about 5,000 ₽. Fees aren't deducted, and the transfer takes up to 10 days.

So a year isn't a blind bet: paying monthly instead costs 1,998 ₽ more on Pro and 998 ₽ more on Basic, and the risk is limited to the days you actually lived through.

Does the week start at registration or when I connect the watch?

At registration in the bot. So it's worth connecting the watch straight away — and with the Historical Data box ticked on the connection page: your past history comes across with it, and the norm is built from that rather than from zero starting today.

What happens when the week ends?

Morning stops arriving, and the bot says so — along with the prices and a button. Nothing is charged: we don't have your card.

"My numbers" stays open after the period ends — the data is yours.

Is the price locked? If it goes up, what about my period?

A new price doesn't touch a period already paid for: changes apply from the date of the new edition of the terms and don't extend to access that is already open. A year bought today lives out at today's price.

Every edition stays at its own address with its date — the one you paid under can always be reopened and reread.

I don't run in winter. If I leave for three months and come back?

Nothing is lost: there are no auto-charges, so simply don't renew. Your history, sport profile and the coach's memory stay, and "My numbers" is open without payment.

What does change is the norm. It's built from recent weeks rather than from your whole life, so after a long gap the coach rebuilds it as you come back. If you kept wearing the watch through it, there'll be plenty to rebuild from.

Can I pay from outside Russia?

Yes. The bot asks about the card before issuing the invoice: a foreign-bank card goes through Robokassa. Prices are in roubles and final either way — the processor's fee is ours to pay.

Where are the official terms?

In the terms of service — a public offer, in Russian, with the prices, the make-up of each plan, and the order of payment, switching and refunds. Every edition stays available at its own address with its date, and changes don't affect a period already paid for. Data handling is in the privacy policy.
