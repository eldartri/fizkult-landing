Explainer 04 · VO2 max.

VO2 max. on a watch: low for the strong, high for the rest

Two independent lab validations agree where their samples overlap: above 55 ml/kg/min the watch reads 3–6 ml/kg/min low, and one of them found it reads about 7% high below 45. On the Cooper Institute table that is one or two rungs, so the error shows up less in the figure than in the word beside it. Where the number comes from, why it freezes over a winter spent indoors, and what the credit line changing from Firstbeat to Garmin inside one month does and doesn't tell you.
