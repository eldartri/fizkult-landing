Version dated 6 September 2026 · Applies in accordance with Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data

This Policy sets out how personal data of Fizkult users (the fizkult.ai website and the @fizkult_ai_bot Telegram bot) is processed.

2. What data is processed

  • Telegram data: user identifier, username, name, language settings;
  • Contact data: email (if you have provided one);
  • Training and physical-condition data from the Garmin account you connect: workouts (type, duration, distance, pace, power), heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, recovery level, weight;
  • Data you tell us yourself: target races, your coach's training plan, medical restrictions, answers in questionnaires;
  • Technical data: logs of your interaction with the bot, the payment status of your subscription (the payment details themselves, such as the card number, are handled by the payment service and are not passed to the operator).

3. Purposes of processing

  • Providing the features of the service: analysing workouts, briefings, recommendations from the AI coach;
  • Setting up and administering a paid subscription;
  • Providing user support and communicating with you;
  • Improving the service (aggregated, de-identified statistics).

4. Legal grounds and consent

Personal data is processed on the basis of your consent, which you give when you connect your Garmin account and start using the bot, and, separately, where processing is necessary to perform the services agreement with you. Data about your health and physical activity is processed solely with your separate consent and only for the purposes of operating the service.

5. Sharing with third parties

Your data is not sold and is not passed to third parties for advertising. Technical contractors (processors) are engaged to operate the service: hosting providers and cloud infrastructure, the provider of the AI model (only the data required to generate a reply is passed to it), and the payment services YooKassa and Robokassa (to take payments; the invoice and the payment page show which of the two is handling your payment). Data may be disclosed to authorised government bodies only in the cases expressly provided for by Russian law.

6. Storage and protection

Data is kept for as long as you use the service and for one year after your account is deleted, unless a longer period is required by law. Credentials for connected services are stored in encrypted form. Access to data is restricted and is granted only for the purposes set out in section 3.

7. Your rights

  • The right to obtain information about how your data is processed;
  • The right to request that your data be corrected, blocked or deleted;
  • The right to withdraw your consent to processing at any time;
  • The right to delete your account and all data associated with it.

To exercise these rights, send a request to hello@fizkult.ai. A request is reviewed within 10 working days. If you withdraw your consent or delete your data, you will no longer be able to use the service.

8. Changes to this policy

The current version is always published at fizkult.ai/privacy.html (in Russian). We tell you about material changes through the bot.
