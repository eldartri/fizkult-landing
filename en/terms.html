1. What the service does

Fizkult is an AI coach for endurance athletes (running, cycling, swimming, triathlon). It works as the Telegram bot @fizkult_ai_bot: it connects to your Garmin watch and takes account of your training, sleep, heart rate, recovery, your plan and your own feedback — and helps you make training decisions. The bot writes in Russian and in English; you pick the language inside the bot with the /language command.

What you get (the functions of the service)

  • Morning — a message every morning: last night's sleep, recovery, the training week and yesterday, read out in a coach's words and set against your own personal norm;
  • Today — the screen for the current day: the same morning picture, which you can come back to during the day;
  • Coach — a conversation with the AI coach on your own data, with memory of previous conversations;
  • Workout analysis — a read of a finished workout: laps, zones, comparison with your norm;
  • Week review — the results of the training week;
  • Sports profile — your list of workouts, your data, and what the service knows about you;
  • My plan — where you put your own training plan so that the coach takes it into account in conversation and in analyses. The service does not write a training plan for you;
  • Team — which coaches can see your data, and how to revoke that access;
  • Watch and data — the state of the connection to Garmin; if the connection breaks, the service tells you itself;
  • Sleep and heart rate — exactly what the service received from the watch and what it did with it.

Which functions are available on each plan is set out in section 2.

Limits of the service. The service is not a medical service, is not a substitute for a doctor and does not give medical advice. Everything the coach says is a coach's reading and the grounds for it; training decisions are yours to make and yours to answer for.

The service is intended for people aged 18 and over.

5. How the service is delivered

The service is digital and is delivered remotely through the Telegram bot. No physical delivery is involved. Once access is open, all the functions of the plan work for the whole of the paid term.

If data stops arriving from the watch

The service runs on the data from your watch and receives it through Garmin Connect. If the flow of data stops, the bot tells you so itself. The subscription term keeps running — but if the paid functions were unavailable for more than two days in a row for a reason on our side or on Garmin's side, write to us: we will extend the term by the number of days of the interruption. You need to write during the paid term or within 14 days after it ends. Interruptions caused by the watch not syncing, or by you revoking the Garmin connection, are not covered by this rule.

Switching between plans inside a paid term

Remaining days are not forfeited in any of the cases below. No paid day is lost, there is no difference to top up, and no refund is made on a switch: we work in days only.

The same plan. The new term is added to what has not yet run out. Example: 20 days remain, you pay for 30 — access runs for 50 days.

Switching to a more expensive plan. You pay for the new plan in full, at its ordinary price. The days remaining from the previous plan are converted into days of the new one in proportion to the prices: the number of remaining days is multiplied by the ratio of the monthly price of the previous plan to the monthly price of the new one, and the result is added to the paid term. A part-day is rounded up, in your favour. Example: you had 20 days of Basic left and you switch to Pro for a month — 20 × (499 ÷ 999) gives 10 days, and Pro runs for 40 days. The new plan starts working immediately.

The conversion is always done using the monthly prices of the plans, regardless of the term you are buying the new plan for. That way the rule means one thing whatever the length of the period.

Switching to a cheaper plan. The previous plan lives out the term already paid for in full — you use what you paid for. The new plan starts when that term ends, and the period you paid for is counted from that moment. No recalculation and no refund takes place: nothing is lost and nothing is topped up.

If the paid term has already ended, switching to any plan is an ordinary purchase: there is nothing to carry over.

If you want the remainder back as money rather than days, that is section 6.

6. Refunds and withdrawal

When you can withdraw

  • Before delivery begins (the subscription is paid for but access has not yet opened) — a refund of the full amount.
  • While the service is being delivered — you may withdraw at any moment. The refund is made in proportion to the unused part of the paid term (art. 32 of the Russian Law on Protection of Consumer Rights, art. 782 of the Russian Civil Code).

How the refund amount is calculated

Refund = amount paid ÷ number of days in the paid term × number of whole days remaining after the day your request is received.

Example: Pro at ₽999 for 30 days, the request arrives on day 10. Twenty whole days remain. The refund is 999 ÷ 30 × 20 = ₽666.

If you switched up to a more expensive plan inside the term, the daily rate is calculated from the price of the plan in force for the term paid (for example ₽999 ÷ 30 days for monthly Pro, or ₽9,990 ÷ 365 days for the annual one), and the remaining days are taken from the resulting term in full, including the days added on the switch. If you switched down to a cheaper plan inside the term and it has not started yet, the remaining term consists of two stretches — the current plan and the one waiting its turn; the refund is calculated for each stretch at its own daily rate, and the amounts are added together. After a refund, access closes immediately — the remaining days are not kept, and a scheduled switch to another plan is cancelled along with them.

The refund is made in the full amount due to you; the payment provider's or the bank's commission is not deducted from it.

There is no need to cancel a renewal separately: there are no automatic charges (section 4).

We answer on working days, usually within a day; the maximum time to answer is 3 working days. The refund period is counted from when the request is received, not from when we answer.
